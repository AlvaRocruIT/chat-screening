Methodologies based on behavioral events have demonstrated considerable validaty when candidates has significative prior experience.
However, in early career stages, they prove to be limited, homogeneous, and weakly diferentiating.

⚡ KEY POINT

Today, it is possible to observe signals that was previously difficult to capture: How candidates build understanding in real time.
Not only through what they ask, but:

  • how they demonstrate a growth mindset
  • how they deepen and conect ideas

  • how their thinking evolves when facing a challenge
    DALMA explores new ways of evaluating early-stage talent by incorporing signals associateded with:
  • growth mindset
  • inquiry capability
  • construction of understanding

    • The focus is not to replace already validated methods, but to complement them with new layers of information where evidence is limited.
